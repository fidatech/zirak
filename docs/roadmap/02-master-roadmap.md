Four tracks run **in parallel**: Business, LMS build, Course production, Channels. Do not wait for the LMS to be finished before making courses or opening channels. Content takes longer than code.

## Phase gates (do not move on until met)

| Gate | Condition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Beta launch | Levels 0–1 done in both languages, reviewed by native editors; LMS stable on a cheap Android phone on 3G | First impressions decide word of mouth |
| Paid launch | 500+ active learners; 30%+ completion of Level 0; HesabPay tested with real small payments | Proves people want it before you ask for money |
| Marketplace | 85+ paying members (break-even); your own review process documented | Protects quality and brand |
| Programs / diplomas | 3 levels complete; at least 50 certificates issued; mentor team in place | A diploma must mean something |
