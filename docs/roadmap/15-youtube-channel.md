# 15 — YouTube channel

**Purpose:** be the best free AI channel in Pashto and Dari, and send viewers to the LMS.

## Setup (week 3)
- [ ] Brand Account under the institute's Google account (not your personal account)
- [ ] Decide on **one channel with two playlists sets** (Pashto / Dari) or **two channels**. Recommendation: start with **one channel** (all growth in one place) with clear language tags in titles: `[پښتو]` / `[دری]`. Split into two channels later if one language clearly dominates.
- [ ] Channel name = brand; handle `@yourbrand`
- [ ] Banner (2560×1440, safe area in the center) with the mascot + "Learn AI in Pashto and Dari, free"
- [ ] Profile picture = logo
- [ ] About section in 3 languages with the website link
- [ ] Links: website, Telegram, Facebook
- [ ] Channel trailer (60 s): who you are, what viewers get, where to get certificates
- [ ] Default upload settings: description template, tags, language, category "Education"
- [ ] Verify the channel (phone) to unlock custom thumbnails and longer videos

## Shows (formats)

| Show | Length | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI This Week** (هوش مصنوعی این هفته / د دې اونۍ مصنوعي ځیرکتیا) | 8–12 min | Weekly, each language | The new tools and updates, explained simply. Competes with English news channels for local viewers |
| **Free course lessons** (Level 0–1) | 5–12 min | 3–5/week | Full free lessons; playlist per course |
| **AI Trick of the Day** (Shorts) | 30–60 s | Daily | Growth, reach |
| **Tool tutorials** | 10–20 min | Weekly | Search traffic ("how to use X in Pashto") |
| **Student stories** (faceless or voice-only, with permission) | 3–5 min | Monthly | Trust, conversion |
| **Live Q&A** (voice + screen) | 45–60 min | Biweekly | Community; announce courses |

## Every video
- Title: clear benefit + language tag, e.g. `[دری] چطور با هوش مصنوعی CV بنویسیم؟ | Write your CV with AI`
- Thumbnail: mascot + 3–4 big words + bright contrast; same style for every video
- First 15 seconds: what they will be able to do
- Description: summary, timestamps, prompts used, link to the full course on the LMS, Telegram link
- Pinned comment: "Get the full course with exercises and a certificate: [link]"
- End screen: next lesson + subscribe
- Subtitles uploaded (your edited ones, not auto)
- Card at the point you mention the course

## Monetization
- YouTube Partner Program requires meeting YouTube's current subscriber and watch-time thresholds; check the requirements in YouTube Studio. Treat ad income as a bonus. **Courses are the business.**
- Use YouTube "Courses" or members-only content later, if available in your region, only for bonus material. Keep certificates on your own LMS.
