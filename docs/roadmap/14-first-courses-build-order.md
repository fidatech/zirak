# 14 — Which courses to build first, in order

Build every course in **both Dari and Pashto**. Counts below are per language.

## Wave 1 — Beta launch (weeks 2–11): FREE

### Course 1: Level 0 — Digital Foundations (≈15 lessons)
| Module | Lessons |
|---|---|
| Your phone | 1. Settings you need · 2. Installing and updating apps safely · 3. Storage and battery |
| Typing | 4. Pashto/Dari keyboard on Android · 5. Voice typing · 6. Copy, paste, share |
| Internet | 7. Mobile data and saving data · 8. Google search well · 9. Email account · 10. Google Drive/files |
| Safety | 11. Scams and fake messages · 12. Strong passwords + 2-step login · 13. Privacy on Facebook/Telegram |
| Apps | 14. Telegram and WhatsApp for learning · 15. How to use this institute app |
| Exam | Final quiz → Completion certificate |

### Course 2: Level 1 — AI Beginner (≈30 lessons)
| Module | Lessons |
|---|---|
| What is AI | What AI is (with Afghan examples) · What AI can and cannot do · Main AI apps (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) · Creating accounts safely |
| Talking to AI | First chat · Asking in Pashto/Dari vs English · The 5-part good prompt · Follow-up questions · Giving examples |
| AI for daily life | Writing letters and applications · Translating · Summarizing a long text · Learning anything with AI · Homework help the right way · Health information (and when to see a doctor) |
| AI for work | Writing a CV · Writing a business message · Making a price list · Planning a week |
| Pictures and voice | Talk to AI by voice · Ask about a photo (plants, products, documents) · Make a simple image |
| Using AI wisely | AI mistakes ("hallucinations") · Fake photos and videos · Privacy: what never to share · Ethics and Islamic values in using AI |
| Project | Solve a real problem from your life with AI and explain it |
| Exam | Final exam → Achievement certificate (exam fee) |

### Short courses (marketing magnets, 1 hour each)

## Wave 2 — Paid launch (weeks 9–16)

Project per module; final exam; Achievement certificate.
