# 17 — Facebook page and group

Facebook is where most Afghans already spend their social media time. Use the **page** for reach and ads, and the **group** for community.

## Page setup (week 3)
- [ ] Create the page from a dedicated business profile; add a second trusted admin
- [ ] Category: Education / Educational Consultant
- [ ] Name = brand; username @yourbrand
- [ ] Profile = logo; cover = mascot + "Learn AI in Pashto and Dari, free"
- [ ] About in 3 languages, website, Telegram/WhatsApp contact
- [ ] Button: "Sign up" → LMS
- [ ] Meta Business Suite connected; Instagram linked
- [ ] Meta Pixel / Conversions API on the LMS (sign-up, purchase events) for ads later

## Group setup
- [ ] One group per language ("Learn AI in Pashto", "Learn AI in Dari") linked to the page
- [ ] Membership questions: language, level, goal (no personal data)
- [ ] Rules (same as Telegram, file 16)
- [ ] Units/guides for free courses
- [ ] Weekly themes: Monday "Prompt challenge", Wednesday "Show your work", Friday "Ask anything"
- [ ] Moderators per language; a female moderator

## Content mix (page)
| Type | Share | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Teach | 50% | Tip image, carousel "5 prompts for shopkeepers", Reels from lessons |
| Prove | 20% | Student results, certificates (with permission), numbers ("2,000 learners") |
| Engage | 20% | Polls, questions, challenges |
| Sell | 10% | Course launch, discount, enrollment deadline |

Post 1–2 times a day; Reels 4–5 times a week (cut from lessons).

## Ads (from the paid launch)
- Start with **$5–$10 a day** on the best-performing Reel; objective: Leads or Sales (with the Pixel)
- Audiences: Afghanistan (by language), Afghan diaspora (interests + languages) for the sponsor offer
- Offer in ads: the free course or the 7-day challenge, not a paid course directly
- Measure cost per sign-up and cost per paying student; stop ads that cost more than a student pays in 2 months

## Live sessions
- Biweekly voice/screen lives (Facebook Live with a screen share, camera off)
- Announce 3 days before; post the replay in the group
