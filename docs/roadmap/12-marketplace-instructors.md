# 12 — Opening the marketplace to instructors (month 6+)

Open the marketplace only after your own Levels 0–2 are strong and you have break-even. Your institute courses set the quality bar everyone else must meet.

## Who you want
- Afghan professionals who use AI in their field (doctors, engineers, accountants, designers, developers)
- Diaspora experts teaching in Pashto or Dari
- Your best graduates (after Level 3 + teacher certification)

## Instructor journey
1. **Apply:** profile, expertise, languages, a 5-minute sample lesson, outline of the proposed course
2. **Review:** your team checks the sample (language, accuracy, audio, teaching). Approve, ask for changes, or reject
3. **Teacher onboarding course** (required, free): your lesson format, recording guide, quiz writing, platform rules
4. **Build the course** in the instructor dashboard
5. **Course review** against the checklist below → publish
6. **Ongoing:** ratings, completion rates, Q&A response time; unpublish courses that fall below the minimums

## Course quality checklist (reviewer)
- [ ] Matches an approved outline; clear outcomes; correct level
- [ ] Audio clear; video at least 720p master; subtitles present
- [ ] Language checked by a native editor
- [ ] Each lesson 5–15 minutes; practice in every module
- [ ] Quizzes with explanations; final exam if it awards an Achievement certificate
- [ ] No copied content; no false claims ("earn $5,000 a month guaranteed")
- [ ] Culturally appropriate examples
- [ ] Facts correct, especially health, law and finance

## Revenue share (suggested starting point)

| Sale comes from | Instructor | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Instructor's own coupon/link | 70% | 30% |
| Platform marketing (catalog, ads, search) | 50% | 50% |
| All-access membership | Pool split by minutes watched in their courses | Platform keeps 50% |
| B2B / organization deals | 40% | 60% |

Payment fees come off the top. Earnings unlock after the 14-day refund window; payouts are monthly (file 09).

## Rules (put them in the Instructor Agreement)
- The instructor owns their content and grants you a license to host and sell it, including in bundles and memberships
- Exclusivity: optional, with a higher share for exclusive courses
- Pricing within platform bands; platform-wide promotions are allowed
- No contacting students to sell outside the platform
- Student data is never shared with instructors (names/nicknames and progress only)
- You can remove content that breaks the rules

See `templates/instructor-agreement-outline.md` for the draft structure. Have a lawyer turn it into a contract.
