# Instructor agreement — outline (for your lawyer)

This is a structure to hand to a lawyer, not a finished contract.

1. **Parties:** [US LLC name] ("Platform") and the Instructor
2. **Definitions:** Course, Content, Net Revenue (after payment fees, taxes, refunds), Student
3. **License:** the Instructor grants a non-exclusive (or exclusive, if chosen), worldwide license to host, stream, sell, translate subtitles for, include in bundles/memberships, and promote the Content
4. **Ownership:** the Instructor keeps ownership; the Platform owns its brand, platform, certificates design and student data
5. **Instructor promises:** original content or licensed material; accuracy; no illegal, hateful, political or misleading content; follows the platform quality checklist
6. **Review and publishing:** nothing goes live without platform approval; the Platform may request changes or unpublish
7. **Pricing:** within platform price bands; platform-wide discounts allowed
8. **Revenue share:** table by sale source (file 12); membership pool rules
9. **Payouts:** monthly, after a 14-day refund window, minimum threshold, payout methods, the Instructor's tax responsibilities
10. **Students and data:** no collection of student contact data; no off-platform selling; privacy rules; special protection rules for women-only groups
11. **Certificates:** issued by the Platform under its certificate policy; the Instructor cannot issue platform certificates outside the system
12. **Support:** the Instructor answers Q&A within [3] days
13. **Term and termination:** either party with [30] days' notice; what happens to enrolled students (keep access)
14. **Liability and indemnity**
15. **Governing law and disputes:** [state of the LLC]; online arbitration option
16. **Language:** English version governs; Pashto/Dari translations provided for understanding
